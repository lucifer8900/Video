# 《灵脉余烬》第一批角色设定图提示词

版本：`lingmai-yujin-character-sheet-v2`  
模式：内置图像生成工具  
用途：项目内角色视觉圣经，不是最终宣传图

## 使用方法

每张图使用“统一基础提示词”加对应角色块。不要在画面中渲染角色姓名或任何标签。角色名只用于文件和资产管理。

## 统一基础提示词

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: cinematic live-action game character visual-bible turnaround sheet
Primary request: Create one wide horizontal three-panel character sheet for an original grounded Chinese cultivation-fantasy interactive film game. The three panels must show exactly the same single adult character with identical facial identity, apparent age, body proportions, hairstyle, outfit, colors, materials, accessories, and condition.
Scene/backdrop: perfectly clean warm neutral-gray seamless studio backdrop, no scenery, no floor clutter.
Style/medium: photorealistic live-action cinematic character concept photography; realistic East Asian facial anatomy; natural skin pores and small imperfections; physically believable layered historical-fantasy garments; real woven fabric, leather, wood, bronze, steel, jade, and paper; restrained supernatural accents.
Composition/framing: a clean wide triptych with clear separation but no drawn panel borders. Left panel: straight-on front half-body portrait from head to below the waist, face and eyes sharply readable. Center panel: strict 90-degree side-profile full-body outfit view, head to shoes fully visible, arms relaxed so sleeves, waist, back structure, trousers or skirt, and footwear can be read. Right panel: straight-on front full-body outfit view, head to shoes fully visible in a neutral production pose. Keep generous padding around every view. No extra poses and no inset objects.
Lighting/mood: soft neutral studio key light with restrained cinematic contrast; natural color; no theatrical colored rim light.
Identity constraints: exactly one character repeated three times; same face in every view; same costume state; no beauty-filter face drift; no age drift; no gender drift; no asymmetrical missing accessories.
World constraints: grounded practical Chinese-inspired historical fantasy, fictional rather than a claim of exact real dynasty; magic is rare and material, not a neon videogame aura.
Constraints: no text, no labels, no calligraphy, no logo, no watermark, no border, no UI, no extra person, no creature companion, no cropped head, no cropped feet, no floating weapon covering the silhouette.
Avoid: anime or manga proportions; stylized 3D doll; plastic skin; modern fashion; modern shoes; cosplay sheen; celebrity or actor likeness; resemblance to any existing film, television, animation, game adaptation, or promotional art; generic glowing xianxia robe; oversized armor; excessive jewelry; sexualized clothing; high heels; random wind; dramatic action pose.
```

## 生成后检查

每张图至少检查：

- 三张是否为同一张脸。
- 正面半身是否清楚呈现眼睛、皮肤和人物性格。
- 中间是否为真正 90 度侧面，而非三分之四角度。
- 右侧全身是否完整显示鞋。
- 服装、腰带、道具和色板是否三图一致。
- 是否误用了明星脸、影视版演员脸或已有动画造型。
- 是否出现文字、水印、额外人物、现代物件或无关魔法特效。
